CORES AI Assistant

LLMs, RAG, output parsing, web scraping

A conversational AI assistant that helps people complete Scottish Adult Disability Payment applications on their own, explaining questions and checking answers in plain language.

Read the case study
  • Problem: many vulnerable adults rely on another person to complete long, complex benefit forms.
  • Approach: OpenAI LLMs with retrieval-augmented generation over official Scottish Government guidance and real application forms, plus output parsing and prompt engineering for dialogue control.
  • Results: about 92% accuracy in question-and-answer testing, with responses in 1.2 to 9.8 seconds. It stayed polite and on task when provoked or derailed.
  • Context: MSc dissertation, University of Stirling.

NTSSOSA UK Portal

Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Vercel

A live membership and governance portal for a UK alumni association: member registration and approvals, automatic membership numbers, finances and expenditure, documents, and online elections.

Read the case study
  • Goal: give the chapter one secure place to manage members, dues, documents and elections.
  • Approach: a role-based web portal with secure sign-in and row-level security on a PostgreSQL database, deployed from GitHub to Vercel.
  • Results: in live use by the chapter, running on free-tier infrastructure.
  • Note: member data is private, so screenshots shown here use demonstration data only.
